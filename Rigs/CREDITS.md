# Rig catalog credits

The rig center catalog (`index.json`) mixes two kinds of entries:

* **Hosted rigs** (`file: *.zip`) — packed in this repository and downloaded
  directly by the app.
* **Linked rigs** (`url: ...`) — open the author's official download page in
  your browser. These are never re-hosted; you always get them from the
  author's own source.

## Reforged feature rigs

The TV, monitor and billboard video screens and the posable mannequin are
made for Mine-imator Reforged (`Tools/generate_rigs.py` regenerates them).

## Takedowns

Rig authors: if you want your rig removed from this catalog (or its entry
changed), open an issue or pull request on
[OmniNodeCo/Mine-imator-Reforged](https://github.com/OmniNodeCo/Mine-imator-Reforged)
and it will be handled promptly.
